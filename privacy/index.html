Policies

Privacy policy

How this club website handles personal information, what we collect, and how to reach us about it.

This notice describes the website as built on 8 September 2026. It is written for visitors in India. Have club counsel review it before the enquiry form goes live or any payment flow is added. Confirm the club email address marked To confirm before launch.

Who is responsible

Rotary Club of Bangalore Bhuvaneshwarinagar (a local Rotary club serving Bengaluru, Karnataka, India) is responsible for personal information collected through this website. This site represents that club only. It does not represent Rotary International, Rotary India, or any other Rotary entity unless expressly stated.

Privacy questions: use the contact form, or email the club once a club-controlled address is published. To confirm club email address.

What this site collects today

Enquiry form (prototype)

The enquiry form on the home page is a front-end prototype. Submitting it does not transmit or store your details on a server. When the club connects a live handler, the form will collect only what is needed to reply: name, email, and your message, and optionally a phone number if you choose to give one. Those details will go to a club-controlled mailbox with restricted access.

We will not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, passport, health, financial, or other sensitive identity details through a basic contact form.

Server and hosting logs

Like most websites, the host may record technical request data such as the page requested, date and time, referring page, browser type, and IP address. That logging is used to deliver the site, keep it available, and investigate abuse. We do not use those logs to build marketing profiles of visitors.

Fonts loaded from Google

This prototype loads Fraunces and Instrument Sans from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google's servers when you open a page. Before launch, the club will self-host these fonts so pages no longer request a third-party font service.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

This site does not set its own cookies, does not run analytics or advertising pixels, and does not embed social plugins, maps, or videos from third parties. If non-essential cookies, analytics, or embeds are added later, this policy will be updated and a clear notice will be shown where required.

How we use enquiry details

When the live form is connected, enquiry details will be used only to:

  • Reply to your question, volunteer interest, partnership idea, or event enquiry
  • Keep a short record of the exchange so the club can follow up responsibly
  • Improve how the club answers common questions, without publishing your personal details

We will not sell personal information. We will not add you to a newsletter or marketing list unless you ask for that explicitly.

Retention

Enquiry messages will be kept only as long as needed to handle the matter and meet any lawful record-keeping duty the club confirms, then deleted or archived under club rules. Exact retention periods will be confirmed by the club secretary before the form goes live.

Sharing

Enquiry details stay with the club officers who need them to reply. They may be shared with another club member, a partner organisation, or a district contact only when that is necessary to answer you or deliver the activity you asked about, and only to the extent needed.

Hosting providers process technical request data as described above. Rotary International is not a recipient of your enquiry form data unless you ask us to involve them.

Children

This website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the contact form. Photographs and stories involving children are covered separately in the photo consent notice.

Your choices

You can ask the club to:

  • Tell you what personal information it holds about you from this website
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete or stop using information that is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected

Use the contact form or the club email once published. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Links to other sites

This site links to Rotary International and may later link to a district site and partners. Those sites have their own privacy practices. This policy covers only this club website.

Security

The live site will be served over HTTPS. Access to the club mailbox that receives enquiries should be limited to approved club officers. No payment-card data is collected on this site.

Changes

If the site starts collecting data in a new way (for example a live form handler, donations, newsletter signup, or analytics), this page will be updated before or at the same time as that change.
