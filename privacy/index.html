Policies

Privacy policy

How Rotary Club of Bangalore Bhuvaneshwarinagar collects, uses, stores and protects personal information through this website.

Last updated: 8 September 2026. Written for visitors in India. Have club counsel review this notice before the enquiry form goes live or any payment flow is added. Confirm items marked To confirm before launch. Do not activate donations until a donation privacy section is added.

Who is responsible

Rotary Club of Bangalore Bhuvaneshwarinagar (a local Rotary club serving Bengaluru, Karnataka, India) is responsible for personal information collected through this website. This site represents that club only. It does not represent Rotary International, Rotary India, or any other Rotary entity unless expressly stated.

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact form, or email the club once a club-controlled address is published. To confirm club email address (club-owned mailbox with at least two authorised officers; do not use a personal or temporary address).

What this site collects today

Enquiry form (prototype)

The enquiry form on the home page is a front-end prototype. Submitting it does not transmit or store your details on a server.

When a live handler is connected, this policy will be updated at the same time to name the provider and the exact flow. The form is intended to collect only what is needed to reply: name, email, and your message, and optionally a phone number if you choose to give one. Those details will go to a club-controlled mailbox with restricted access.

We do not ask for Aadhaar, PAN, passport, health, financial, or other sensitive identity details through a basic contact form.

Server and hosting logs

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical request data, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps, and referring pages, to operate, secure, and diagnose the service. The club does not use this information to create marketing profiles of visitors.

Fonts loaded from Google

This site currently loads Fraunces and Instrument Sans from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google's servers when you open a page. Before production launch, these fonts should be self-hosted so pages no longer request a third-party font service. Until then, the Google Fonts request is an active third-party connection.

Images loaded from Unsplash

Some project images on the home page are currently loaded from the Unsplash CDN (images.unsplash.com). Your browser may contact Unsplash's servers when those images load. Before production launch, club-owned or club-licensed images should be self-hosted. Until then, those Unsplash requests are active third-party connections.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

This site does not set its own cookies, does not run analytics or advertising pixels, and does not embed social plugins, maps, or videos from third parties. If non-essential cookies, analytics, or embeds are added later, this policy will be updated with the provider and cookie details, and a clear notice will be shown where required.

How we use enquiry details

When the live form is connected, enquiry details will be used only to:

  • Reply to your question, volunteer interest, partnership idea, or event enquiry
  • Keep a short record of the exchange so the club can follow up responsibly
  • Improve how the club answers common questions, without publishing your personal details

We do not sell personal information. We do not add you to a newsletter or marketing list unless you ask for that explicitly.

Retention

Until the club secretary confirms a final schedule, the intended approach is: enquiry messages are deleted within 12 months after the matter is closed, unless a longer period is required for a specific event, donation, legal matter, or ongoing relationship. Exact retention rules will be confirmed before the form goes live. To confirm retention schedule.

Sharing

Enquiry details stay with the club officers who need them to reply. We share an enquiry with a club member, partner organisation, or district contact only where it is necessary to respond to the request or deliver the activity requested, and only to the extent needed. Where a referral or sharing is not reasonably expected from the enquiry, we will ask for your permission first.

GitHub Pages and related hosting infrastructure process technical request data as described above. Rotary International is not a recipient of your enquiry form data unless you ask us to involve them.

Children

This website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the contact form. Photographs and stories involving children are covered separately in the photo consent notice.

Your choices

You can ask the club to:

  • Tell you what personal information it holds about you from this website
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete or stop using information that is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected

Use the contact form or the club email once published. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

Links to other sites

This site links to Rotary International and may later link to a district site and partners. Those sites have their own privacy practices. This policy covers only this club website.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS on GitHub Pages. Access to the club mailbox that receives enquiries should be limited to approved club officers. No payment-card data is collected on this site. Do not add donation, UPI, ticket, or payment flows until this policy is updated with the receiving entity, payment provider, purpose, receipt process, and donor-data rules.

Changes

If the site starts collecting data in a new way (for example a live form handler, donations, newsletter signup, or analytics), this page will be updated before or at the same time as that change, including the last-updated date.
