Photo consent notice

How we treat photographs, video and other images that show identifiable people on this club website.

Last updated 8 September 2026. This notice follows the club website rules on media, children, and dignity. It is not a substitute for written consent forms the club keeps on file.

Our approach

Rotary Club of Bangalore Bhuvaneshwarinagar publishes photographs and video to show real community work. We prefer club-owned or club-licensed images. We do not treat stock photographs as proof of our own projects.

Before we publish an image in which a person can reasonably be identified, we obtain informed, written consent from that person, or from a parent or legal guardian when the person is a child.

What we ask consent for

  • Photographs and video used on this website
  • Images reused in club social posts that point back to this site, when the same identifiable frame is involved
  • Quotations, testimonials, and case studies tied to an identifiable person

Consent should say how the image may be used, where it may appear, and any time limit or restriction the person asks for.

Children and young people

For children, we obtain consent from a parent or legal guardian and follow the event host or school's policy where one applies. We do not publish a child's full name together with a school routine, exact home location, medical details, or other information that could create risk.

Dignity and health content

We do not use distress, illness, disability, poverty, or disaster imagery to sensationalise an appeal. We do not publish patient information, diagnoses, prescriptions, or medical records. Health-camp coverage names the responsible clinical organisation where appropriate and stays within a careful scope.

What is on the site today

Some images on the current prototype are still placeholders:

  • The hero artwork is an illustration, not a photograph of members or beneficiaries, and is labelled as such
  • Focus-area photographs may still be licensed stock standing in for club images
  • The featured project slot stays empty until the club has a consented project photograph

Those stand-ins will be replaced with consented club media before launch wherever an identifiable person is shown.

Media register

The club keeps an internal media register for published images. It records source, owner, licence, consent status, date, subject or project, and any expiry or restriction. That register is not public.

Members and leadership

Leadership names and photographs appear only with the individual's consent and confirmation from the club secretary. Personal phone numbers, home addresses, and member-only documents are not published on this site.

Asking us to remove an image

If you appear in an image on this website and want it reviewed or removed, contact us through the contact form or the club email once published (To confirm). Please include a link to the page and a description of the image. We will take reasonable steps to remove or replace it where consent is withdrawn or was never given, subject to any legal or archival duty the club must meet.

Events and group settings

At public club events, people may be photographed as part of documenting the activity. Where practical, the club will give notice at the event. Anyone who prefers not to be photographed should tell an organising member so the team can take reasonable steps to avoid identifiable shots of them.
