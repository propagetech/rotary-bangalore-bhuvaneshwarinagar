Who we are

Service that starts by listening.

A club is only as useful as the questions it asks first.

We are members of Rotary International, working in and around Bengaluru. Some of us are teachers, doctors, engineers and business owners. What we share is a habit of turning up, and a preference for work that can be checked.

Meet the club

  • We ask before we act

    We begin with the people closest to the problem, not with a plan we brought with us. A short conversation with a headmistress, a nurse or a ward office usually reshapes the whole project.

  • We finish what we start

    Small, clear commitments with a named owner, a date and a follow up visit. We would rather do four things properly than announce fourteen.

  • We report honestly

    Numbers we can evidence, photographs we have permission to publish, and names only with consent. Where we do not yet have a figure, we say so instead of rounding one up.

What we work on

Four areas where a local club can genuinely move things.

Each of these is chosen because a club our size can make a measurable difference in it without waiting for anyone else.

01

Education

Scholarships, classroom and toilet repair, learning material, library and digital equipment, and mentoring for students who are one small support away from staying in school.

Tell us about a school that needs help
02

Health

Screening and eye camps, blood donation drives, preventive awareness, and help for families navigating care they would otherwise go without.

Partner on a health camp
03

Environment

Tree planting with a watering plan attached, lake and street clean ups, waste segregation drives, and environmental education inside schools.

Join a planting or clean up
04

Livelihood

Vocational training, tools and equipment, career guidance and small business support. The aim is narrow and practical: turn a skill somebody already has into an income they can rely on.

  • Tailoring and trade kits
  • Computer and spoken English
  • Interview coaching
  • Seed capital for a first stall

Get involved

There is a practical way in for almost everyone.

You do not need to join the club to work with it. Most people start with one project and decide from there.

  1. Tell us what you can offer

    An evening a month, a professional skill, a venue, transport, equipment, funding, or simply a community need you know about and we do not.

  2. We match you to live work

    A club member points you at a project that is actually running, with a named contact, a real date and a clear idea of what is needed from you.

  3. Show up and do the work

    A short briefing, defined roles, and a follow up afterwards so you can see what your time actually changed.
