Accessibility statement

We aim to make this club website usable by as many people as possible. Here is what we are working toward and how to tell us when something gets in the way.

Last updated 8 September 2026. This statement covers the Rotary Club of Bangalore Bhuvaneshwarinagar website only.

Our commitment

Rotary Club of Bangalore Bhuvaneshwarinagar intends this website to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the implementation target. We design for keyboard use, readable contrast, clear headings, meaningful image text alternatives, and visible focus states.

What we build for

  • One clear h1 per page and a logical heading order
  • Skip link to main content
  • Colour contrast suitable for body text, links, and buttons on light and dark sections
  • Meaningful alt text for informative images; empty alt for decorative images
  • Form fields with visible labels and understandable error messages
  • Motion that respects prefers-reduced-motion
  • Layouts that work on small screens without requiring sideways scrolling for main content

Known limitations

This site is still being prepared for launch. Current limitations we are tracking:

  • Fonts currently load from Google Fonts; they will be self-hosted before launch
  • Some project photographs are still placeholders or licensed stock standing in for club images
  • Some contact details remain marked To confirm until the club supplies them
  • The enquiry form is a prototype and does not yet send messages

We will update this statement as those items are closed.

Compatibility

Pages are built as semantic HTML with progressive enhancement. Core content and navigation should remain available if JavaScript fails or is turned off. We test in current versions of major desktop and mobile browsers. Assistive technology support is part of our review process; it is not a claim that every combination has been certified.

Reporting a barrier

If you find content that is hard to use, cannot be reached with a keyboard, lacks a text alternative, or fails in your assistive technology, please tell us.

  • Use the contact form on the home page
  • Or email the club once a club-controlled address is published (To confirm)

Please include the page URL, what you were trying to do, and the device or browser if you know it. We will acknowledge reports and aim to reply within a few working days once the live mailbox is connected.

Formal feedback

Accessibility feedback is reviewed by the club officers responsible for this website. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may also raise the matter with the club president or secretary through the club's published contact routes.
